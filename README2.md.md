# Superstore Sales Data Analysis

## Data Analytics Internship Project

This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data using Python to identify important sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, regional patterns, and order trends.

The project was completed as part of a Data Analytics Internship and demonstrates practical skills in data inspection, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, and business-oriented interpretation.

---

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze Superstore sales data and derive meaningful business insights.

The analysis covers:

- Overall sales performance
- Regional sales performance
- Category and sub-category performance
- Yearly and monthly sales trends
- Top and bottom-performing products
- Customer segment performance
- High-value customers
- Order trends
- Business insights and recommendations

## Key Results

### Overall Performance

- **Total Sales:** $2,261,536.78
- **Unique Orders:** 4,922

### Customer Segments

| Segment | Total Sales | Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| Consumer | $1,148,060.53 | 50.76% |
| Corporate | $688,494.07 | 30.44% |
| Home Office | $424,982.18 | 18.79% |

### Yearly Performance

| Year | Sales | Orders |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2015 | $479,856.21 | 947 |
| 2016 | $459,436.01 | 1,019 |
| 2017 | $600,192.55 | 1,295 |
| 2018 | $722,052.02 | 1,661 |

2018 was the strongest year in terms of both sales and order volume.

### Regional Product Performance

| Region | Top Sub-category | Sales |
|---|---|---:|
| West | Chairs | $100,023.20 |
| East | Phones | $99,884.66 |
| Central | Chairs | $82,372.78 |
| South | Phones | $58,098.34 |

## Key Business Insights

- Consumer customers generated more than half of total sales.
- Sales increased substantially from 2016 to 2018.
- 2018 recorded the highest sales and order volume.
- Order volume increased from 947 in 2015 to 1,661 in 2018.
- Product performance varied significantly across individual products.
- Product demand differed across geographical regions.
- High-value customers represent potential opportunities for customer retention.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Continue strengthening the Consumer customer segment.
- Develop targeted strategies for Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Use region-specific product strategies based on observed demand.
- Focus on retaining high-value customers.
- Investigate low-performing products before making inventory decisions.
- Monitor sales and order trends regularly.
- Analyze average sales per order to better understand purchasing behavior.
